CodeExplainedBadly — Automated YouTube Publisher

This application is a private, first-party tool that publishes short-form and long-form videos to the operator's own YouTube channels (a computer-science / cloud education channel and a gaming channel). It runs on the operator's own machine and uploads content the operator produced.

What it does

  • Uploads videos and Shorts to the operator's own YouTube channels on their behalf.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, and privacy status for those uploads.

Who it's for

This is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to manage their own channels. It is not a public multi-user service.

Google account access

The app uses Google OAuth with the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube solely to upload and manage the owner's own videos. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
